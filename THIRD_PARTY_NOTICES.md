## Important notes

- This summary is informational and is not a substitute for the complete upstream licence and notice files shipped with the application.
- ONNX Runtime's `LICENSE` and `ThirdPartyNotices.txt` should be distributed verbatim.
- GEOS and FFmpeg are LGPL components. Their licence texts, replacement rights, and corresponding-source information must remain available to recipients.
- The bundled RapidOCR model files come from RapidOCR 3.9.2. Their provenance and any model-specific terms should be rechecked for each public release.
- Some transitive dependencies are not individually pinned in the requirements files. Regenerate and verify this inventory for every release build.
- PyMuPDF, listed in `requirements-pixel.txt`, is not bundled in the current Windows application. If distributed later, its AGPL-3.0-or-commercial terms require separate review.
